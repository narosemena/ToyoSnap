# ToyoSnap — Tester Brief

Thanks for agreeing to test ToyoSnap. This page tells you what it is, what I'm asking you to do, and how your feedback will be used.

---

## What ToyoSnap is

ToyoSnap is a browser extension for instructional designers. It records what you do on a web page and turns that recording into training-ready artifacts — videos, step-by-step image chains, interactive HTML walkthroughs, layered SVGs, and an auto-generated design system document.

It runs **entirely on your machine.** Nothing you record ever leaves your computer. No cloud, no analytics, no phone-home.

---

## What I'm asking you to do

Spend about **45–60 minutes** using the extension on tasks I'll give you, while I watch and take notes. Afterwards, you'll fill out a short feedback form.

You'll go through 6 scenarios, each 3–10 minutes. I'll introduce each one, then step back and let you work. I won't help unless you're completely stuck. **Please think aloud as you work** — say what you're trying to do, what you expect to happen, and what confuses you.

---

## Ground rules

- **Capture non-sensitive workflows only.** The tool keeps your data local, but we don't need real customer data or real passwords in test recordings. Use dummy data or public sites.
- **Get frustrated out loud.** If something annoys you, say so. Don't try to be polite — polite feedback is useless feedback.
- **You're testing the tool, not yourself.** If something doesn't work, that's the tool's problem, not yours.
- **Feel free to quit any task.** Tell me why and we'll move on.

---

## How your feedback is used

Every piece of feedback gets triaged into a severity bucket and fed into the fix queue. I'll tell you in writing what changed as a result of your round before the tool ships.

Your session notes and quotes may be referenced anonymously in internal documentation. Your name won't appear anywhere unless you explicitly say it's OK.

---

## Consent

By starting the session, you agree to:
- Let me record the screen and audio (for my own review only)
- Let me quote you anonymously in triage docs
- That you'll capture only non-sensitive workflows during testing

If any of that doesn't work for you, tell me now and we'll adjust.

---

## Before we start

- Browser: Chrome or Chromium-based (Edge, Brave, Arc work)
- OS: Windows, macOS, or Linux
- I'll send you the install package and a one-page install guide separately
- Please install it and confirm the extension icon appears in your toolbar before our session
